Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Introduction

"Today's Ingredients" (hereinafter "the App") is a mobile application provided by suuan (hereinafter "we" or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data within the App.

About the App

The App displays nutritional values of food ingredients based on the Standard Tables of Food Composition in Japan (8th Edition) and the Dietary Reference Intakes for Japanese (2025 Edition), tailored to the user's age and sex.

Data We Collect

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data only on your device (local storage). This data is never transmitted to our servers.

  • User preferences: Sex, age group, activity level, menstruation status (used to calculate recommended nutrient intakes)
  • Food memo: Food ingredient memo data created by the user
  • Language setting: Japanese or English display preference
  • Purchase status: Ad-free plan purchase state

Data Collected Directly by Us

The App does not transmit or collect any personal information to our servers.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services. These services may collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect information such as device identifiers (advertising ID), IP address, and approximate location for ad delivery and optimization.

Details: Google Privacy Policy

RevenueCat (In-App Purchase Management)

The App uses RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases (ad-free plan). RevenueCat may collect purchase information, anonymous app user IDs, and device information.

Details: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Advertising ID Usage

The App may use the Google Advertising ID (GAID / IDFA) for personalized ad delivery. You can reset or disable the advertising ID through your device settings.

Affiliate Links

The App contains affiliate links to the following services. If you purchase products through these links, we may receive a referral commission.

  • Rakuten Affiliate (search links to Rakuten Ichiba)
  • Amazon Associates (search links to Amazon Fresh) *application pending

Using affiliate links does not change the purchase price for you.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your data to third parties, except for the automatic collection by the third-party services described above.

Data Deletion

All data stored by the App can be deleted by:

  • Using "Reset All Settings" in the App's Settings
  • Uninstalling the App from your device

To delete data associated with your advertising ID, please reset the advertising ID through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children under 13. The App provides nutritional information and can be used by anyone regardless of age. If a child under 13 uses the App, we recommend parental supervision.

Medical Disclaimer

Nutritional information provided by the App is for general informational purposes only and is not a substitute for medical advice or diagnosis. For specific questions about diet or health, please consult a physician or registered dietitian.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as needed. If significant changes are made, we will notify you within the App. The latest version is always available on this page.
